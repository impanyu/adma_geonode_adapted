Yield Summary Tool

Analyze treatment sectors and yield data with statistical analysis

This tool processes treatment sector and yield shapefiles to generate:

  • Buffer Shapefile - Buffered treatment polygons for spatial analysis
  • Summary Shapefile - Merged spatial result with yield statistics
  • Summary Excel - Tabular summary of results
  • Statistics Excel - ANOVA analysis and treatment comparison (NUE, PFP, MNR)
